Kitchen Language Review

Every word the Happy Chair Kitchen Display shows a cook, in English, draft Spanish (written for Mexican and U.S. restaurant kitchens) and draft Simplified Chinese. Cooks read this screen at a distance, during service, sometimes during an anaphylaxis alert. Please judge each line by one question: would a cook who reads this language do exactly the right thing after reading it?
