Kitchen Spanish Review

Every word the Happy Chair Kitchen Display shows a cook, in English and in the current draft Spanish. Cooks read this screen at a distance, during service, sometimes during an anaphylaxis alert. Please judge each line by one question: would a Spanish-speaking cook do exactly the right thing after reading it?

Keep the allergen meaning“Cross-contact” means a trace of the allergen reaching the food through shared surfaces, utensils, oil, gloves or hands. It is not only bacterial contamination.
